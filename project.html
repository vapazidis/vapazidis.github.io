Baby Language

This is an interpreter and calculator written in Python that has custom, baby-themed syntax. It was the capstone of our Organization of Programming Languages class.

Our class worked together on each individual part of the program as part of our class time.

The program's error reporting is rather robust, and it can handle complicated nested equations. It can do essentially anything a simple calculator can do. (But definitely not everything a baby can do.)

See the repository here.

"Fridgebot"

This Discord bot, written entirely in Python, was my first project in Computer Science. It stored images, allowed its users to tag the input, and then retrieve art that falled under a certain tag.

Back in 2021, an art circle I was in needed a way to automatically archive art posted to our Discord server. Eager to solve a problem, I created a Discord bot to facilitate this archival, and affectionately named it "Fridgebot"!

While all it could do initially was archive and post to a designated channel, I eventually gave it tagging and retrieval commands. Without knowing it, I had created a rudimentary database of art for my friends to use at their leisure.

Thank you, Fridgebot, for everything you taught me.
